Project 02SoftwareComplete · Sep 2026

Coilgun Timing Simulator

The hardware came first. Now, the model.

A Python physics simulator coupling capacitor discharge to projectile motion through a position-dependent inductance, solved with a custom RK4 integrator. It found that 46.8% of my muzzle velocity was being lost to suckback — and where to move the projectile to get most of it back.

Electromagnetic Accelerator (Coil Gun)
Jun. 2022 – Present

Individual Project

  • Designed and built a two-stage coil gun, solving the challenge of precisely timing sequential electromagnetic coils to efficiently accelerate a projectile.
  • Used ANSYS Maxwell 2D for electromagnetic field simulation and coil-timing optimization, Fusion 360 for mechanical design, 3D printing for fabrication, and Arduino for stage-control logic.
  • Achieved approximately 11 m/s projectile velocity with a 54.64 g projectile using four 400 V, 330 µF capacitors.
Coilgun Timing Simulator
Aug. 2026 – Sep. 2026

Individual Project

  • Built a Python simulator coupling capacitor-discharge circuit dynamics to projectile motion through a position-dependent inductance model, solving the resulting 5-variable coupled ODE system with a custom 4th-order Runge-Kutta integrator.
  • Validated numerically rather than by fitting: matched the closed-form RLC solution, confirmed 4th-order convergence (16.0× error reduction per step halving), and enforced energy conservation to 6×10⁻¹⁴ relative across circuit, kinetic, and resistive terms.
  • Automated 22 tests across 3 Python versions in GitHub Actions; quantified a 46.8% velocity loss to magnetic suckback and identified a projectile-placement change predicted to recover 37% of muzzle velocity.
Arduino Sensor & Control System
Jun. 2026 – Jul. 2026

Individual Project

  • Implemented I2C communication between the Arduino, sensors, and OLED display.
