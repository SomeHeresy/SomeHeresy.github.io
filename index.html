ElectromagneticsEmbedded controlCAD

Electromagnetic Accelerator

A two-stage coil gun I’ve been rebuilding since 2022. The hard part was never the voltage — it was firing each coil at the right point in the slug’s travel, which took ANSYS Maxwell 2D to model, Fusion 360 and 3D-printed supports to hold alignment, and Arduino stage-control logic to actually execute.

Result
~16 m/s

I ran R&D and simulation for a seven-person team building a 25.6-inch competition rocket. Iterating the OpenRocket model against how the vehicle actually behaved tightened our predictions by 23.2%, and it flew two qualification flights for an official score of 139.8.

Arduino Sensor & Control System

An Uno R3 reading an ultrasonic distance sensor and a temperature/humidity sensor, redrawing both over I²C to an OLED every 50 ms. Small build, but it’s where the timing side of embedded work stopped being abstract — the loop has to stay honest while the display keeps up.
